# Apex Horizon — Design Direction

## Product

A fast, readable, one-thumb-friendly road-racing sprint for portrait mobile screens. The experience should capture the accessible fantasy of a premium festival racer without copying any existing game's branding, cars, UI, locations, or assets.

## Visual language

- Deep navy UI plates over a vivid coastal road.
- Electric cyan communicates event identity and route progress.
- Warm yellow identifies the player, nitro, checkpoints, and important outcomes.
- Chunky condensed-feeling type, large speed readout, and sparse telemetry create a motorsport broadcast character.
- Cars and scenery are procedural vector art, keeping the build lightweight and original.

## Gameplay pillars

1. **Immediate speed:** automatic cruising means the race starts without setup.
2. **Expressive control:** steering, throttle, brake, and nitro provide a small but meaningful skill surface.
3. **Readable competition:** rivals occupy clear lanes, race position is always visible, and passes produce feedback.
4. **Mobile-first:** drag steering and a large boost button work without a virtual joystick.
5. **Short-session finish:** one 3.2 km sprint provides a complete start, checkpoint, and results loop.

## Interaction rules

- Road shoulders slow the car but do not hard-stop it.
- Contact is recoverable and clearly signaled.
- Checkpoints and clean passes replenish nitro to reward active racing.
- Keyboard and touch controls remain equally viable.
